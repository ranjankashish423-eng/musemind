MuseMind – AI Personal Journal

An AI-powered journaling platform that detects emotions from text and provides personalized reflections with mood analytics.

Problem Statement

Many students struggle with stress and emotional imbalance. Existing journaling apps only store entries without emotional understanding. There is a need for an AI-based system that analyzes emotions and provides meaningful feedback.

Literature Review

Apps like Reflectly and Daylio provide basic mood tracking but lack deep AI-based emotion insights.

Research Gap

MuseMind integrates emotion detection, reflection generation, and mood visualization in one platform.

Proposed Methodology

Input

User journal entries

AI Processing

Emotion detection + reflection

Output

Dashboard visualization
